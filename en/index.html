Light on Them

Discover the forgotten women of science

This site highlights the forgotten women scientists of history. For centuries, their discoveries have been attributed to their male colleagues, a phenomenon called the Matilda effect. Through articles, a timeline and analyses, discover these pioneers who revolutionized science but whose names have been erased from history books. It's time to do them justice.
